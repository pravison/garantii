4. HOW THE ESCROW PROCESS WORKS
  • Step 1 – Payment: Buyer deposits funds into escrow.
  • Step 2 – Notification: Seller is notified to deliver the product/service.
  • Step 3 – Confirmation: Buyer confirms receipt.
  • Step 4 – Release: Funds are released to the seller.
  • Step 5 – Disputes: Funds remain in escrow until resolved.
